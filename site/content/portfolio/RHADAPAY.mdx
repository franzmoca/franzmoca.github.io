### Project Description

I participated in HackFS, a two-week hackathon organized by EthGlobal. Our team created a proof of concept for RhadaPay, an event-driven payment platform leveraging blockchain technology.

The platform enables payment streams that are directly linked to real-world events. I was primarily responsible for integrating Superfluid, a protocol for payment streaming. I developed a smart contract that adjusts the amount paid every second in response to off-chain events, providing a dynamic and responsive payment solution.

### Contributions

- **Superfluid Integration:** Developed smart contracts that interact with the Superfluid protocol, enabling real-time adjustments to payment streams based on external triggers.
- **Smart Contract Development:** Created contracts that manage the logic for streaming payments, ensuring secure and efficient transactions on the blockchain.
- **Collaboration:** Worked closely with an international team across four time zones, contributing to both the technical development and project planning.
