In February 2023, I earned the **Professional Scrum Developer I (PSD I)** certification from Scrum.org.

This certification reflects my ongoing effort to deepen my understanding of Agile methodologies and improve my skills as a software developer within Scrum teams.

The PSD I certification focuses on the practical application of Scrum principles in software development. The process involved studying various aspects of the Scrum framework, including the roles, artifacts, and events that are integral to its implementation. It also tested my ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams and manage the development process in an Agile environment.

Gaining this certification has provided me with a clearer perspective on how to contribute to Scrum teams and apply Agile practices more effectively in my work. It’s a step forward in my professional development, adding to the tools and techniques I use to manage projects and collaborate with others.

Not always Agile methodologies are the best fit for every project, but having a solid understanding of Scrum principles and practices can help me adapt to different situations and make informed decisions about how to approach software development projects.
