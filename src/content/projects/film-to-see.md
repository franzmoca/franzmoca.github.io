A simple Android app that solves the classic problem: a group of friends can't decide what movie to watch.

Each user adds films they want to see to a shared list. The app displays the list to all group members and lets them vote, surfacing the most popular pick. Built with Java and Firebase Realtime Database for live syncing across devices.
