Snafu Collective is a decentralized NFT marketplace built on the xDai chain (now Gnosis Chain), focused on making digital art accessible without Ethereum mainnet gas costs.

The project used ERC1155 multi-token standard to allow artists to mint both unique and edition-based NFTs. A custom staking mechanism let collectors earn yield on held tokens, adding a DeFi layer to the marketplace.

Key technical components included a custom Solidity marketplace contract, IPFS for artwork storage, The Graph for indexing events, and a React frontend with Web3Modal for wallet connection.
