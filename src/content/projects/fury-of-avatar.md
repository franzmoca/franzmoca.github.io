A web-based deckbuilder and game companion for a custom trading card game prototype, built during the summer of 2015.

The app let players browse the card catalog, build decks, and simulate matches against the AI or another player. Built with PHP, MySQL, jQuery, and Bootstrap — a classic LAMP stack project that was my first serious web development work.
