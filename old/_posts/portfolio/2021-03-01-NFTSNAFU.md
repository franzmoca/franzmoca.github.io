### Project Description

I am collaborating with an Italian collective of artists experimenting with NFTs. My role involves developing parts of the [NFT marketplace](https://app.nftsnafu.org) and contributing to the creation of the [smart contract](https://github.com/SNAFU-Collective/snafu-smartcontracts) for the project, which is deployed on the xDaiChain public blockchain.

This project also includes a DeFi component: each NFT can be converted into an ERC20 token ($SNAFU) based on its rarity, adding a unique financial layer to the art marketplace.

### Contributions

- **NFT Marketplace Development:** Assisted in building the front-end and smart contract integration for the NFT marketplace, allowing artists to mint and trade NFTs.
- **Smart Contract Implementation:** Developed ERC1155 and ERC20 smart contracts, ensuring secure and efficient tokenization and conversion processes.
- **DeFi Component:** Integrated a DeFi feature that links NFT rarity to an ERC20 token, enabling dynamic pricing and exchangeability within the platform.
