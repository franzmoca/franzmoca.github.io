### Project Description

**BlockchainVis** is a data visualization tool developed as part of my bachelor thesis from February 2016 to April 2016. The application was designed to track and visualize the flow of Bitcoin transactions, providing insights into the movement of funds across the blockchain. This tool was particularly focused on forensic transaction analysis.

My primary role in this project was centered around the backend development. This involved scraping data from the blockchain and loading it into a graph database, enabling the complex visualization of transaction flows. The application’s frontend utilized d3.js to present this data in an interactive and insightful manner.

### Contributions

Key contributions to the **BlockchainVis** project included:

- Developing the backend infrastructure using Node.js, PHP, and OrientDB to scrape and store Bitcoin transaction data.
- Implementing an MVC architecture to separate concerns within the application, ensuring maintainability and scalability.
- Utilizing AJAX and jQuery to enable dynamic interactions within the application.
- Designing the data visualization using d3.js, allowing users to explore the flow of transactions through an intuitive graphical interface.

### Challenges Overcame

Several challenges were encountered during the development of **BlockchainVis**:

- **Data Scraping Complexity:** Effectively scraping and parsing large volumes of blockchain data in real-time.
- **Database Management:** Choosing and managing a graph database (OrientDB) to efficiently store and retrieve complex transaction data.
- **Visual Representation:** Designing a clear and effective visualization to represent the intricate flow of Bitcoin transactions.

### Accomplishments

The **BlockchainVis** project culminated in a robust and functional tool that:

- Enabled users to track and analyze Bitcoin transactions visually, aiding in forensic analysis and research.
- Provided a foundation for future research and development, as demonstrated by subsequent academic work based on this project.
- Was successfully defended as part of my bachelor thesis, contributing to my academic achievements and sparking further interest in blockchain technologies.
