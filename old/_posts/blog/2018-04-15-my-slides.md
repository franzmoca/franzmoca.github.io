Throughout my university studies, I had the opportunity to delve deep into various subjects by preparing and presenting slides on specific topics for different courses. 

These assignments allowed me to not only explore areas of personal interest but also to hone my presentation and research skills, critical for effective communication in both academic and professional settings.

Below, I’ve shared some of the most impactful presentations I created during my university career. These slides represent my learning journey and my efforts to explore complex topics within the realm of computer science.

#### [Information Diffusion in Online Social Networks](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1fYZyWVFhVZSivq6UZz0wSKH4JvsOhOqYw7OczCPP9PQ/edit?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} - *Data Mining Exam*

In this presentation, I explored the key concepts of information diffusion within online social networks. The slides cover fundamental models such as the Independent Cascade Model and the Linear Threshold Model, which are used to simulate how information spreads across networks. Understanding these concepts is crucial for analyzing viral content, influencer impact, and the dynamics of social media platforms.

#### [Pony: A New Approach to Concurrent Programming](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1tVxvadWEhFBBeIu-kAlnfKKeGcUfZpB4aM8tT7wz5J0/edit?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} - *Emerging Programming Paradigms Exam*

This set of slides introduces the Pony programming language, an innovative language designed for writing safe and efficient concurrent programs. Pony leverages the actor model to manage concurrency without data races, offering a novel approach to solving problems in modern software development. The presentation delves into the core features of Pony, such as reference capabilities and garbage collection, and discusses its potential applications.

#### [Ethereum and ÐApps: The Future of Decentralized Computing](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1-uGFfivmRUoPol8JuuO4ppK4g7Xu0MsdxNnJRAW8ERw/edit?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"} - *Peer To Peer Systems Exam*

This presentation provides an introduction to Ethereum, a blockchain-based platform that enables the development of Decentralized Applications (ÐApps). The slides explain the underlying technology of Ethereum, its smart contract functionality, and explore various ÐApps that were prominent in 2017. Understanding Ethereum is essential for grasping the broader implications of blockchain technology in reshaping industries and fostering decentralized innovation.
